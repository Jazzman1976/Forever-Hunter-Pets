# Code of Conduct

Forever Hunter Pets is a small hobby project for hunters in WoW Forever. Everybody is welcome here.

## In short

- **Be friendly and respectful** – to players, contributors and the maintainer.
- **Stay on topic** – the addon, its data and its development.
- **Report what you saw in the game** – no data from websites or other addons.

## Not okay

Insults, harassment, discrimination, spam, advertising, trolling and deliberately false reports.

## What happens

The maintainer may hide or delete comments, edit or close issues, lock conversations and block people who break these rules – without prior warning if needed. If something bothers you, mention it in an issue or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
